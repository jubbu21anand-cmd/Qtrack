How to Use
1
Add people — tap "+ Person" in the header. Each person gets their own bar on the chart and their own card below.
2
Add subjects — on any person's card, tap the Subjects button. Add whatever subjects you study, pick a colour for each, and tap Save. Subjects are shared across all people in your session.
3
Log questions — enter how many you did per subject and tap Add, then tap ✓ Log done at the bottom of the card once you're finished for the day to lock it in.
4
Connect a friend — tap ⚡ in the header, copy your Session ID, and send it. Your friend pastes it in "Join a Session" and hits Join. Both boards sync live.
5
Track progress — use the chart views (Today / By Day / Week / Month / All-Time), the calendar, and the Leaderboard tab. Week view has ‹ › arrows to browse previous weeks; Month view has a dropdown to jump straight to any month.
6
Go anonymous — tap the 🕶 icon on your card to hide your numbers from every other device in the session. Only your own device keeps showing your real progress; everyone else just sees a locked "Anonymous" card. Tap 🕶 again on the same device to turn it back off.
7
Study Tracker — open it from the ☰ menu. Tap a person's name to give them a ring timer. Choose Stopwatch to count up freely, Timer to count down a fixed number of minutes, or Pomodoro for the classic work/break cycle — set your work length and QTrack automatically gives a break (with a long break every 4th round) and flashes it on screen. Several people can run their own timers on the same device at once. Focus time saves automatically to that person's Focus Calendar and weekly chart.
8
Exam Analytics — open it from the ☰ menu. Pick a mode at the top (JEE / NEET / Boards / Other) so competitive and board exam scores never get mixed into the same average. Tap + Add Test, enter total marks, marks obtained, rank, total candidates appeared, and a per-subject breakdown — percentile is calculated for you. Every test becomes its own colour-graded card (green = strong, yellow = average, red = weak) with JM/JA badges for JEE Main/Advanced mocks, editable and openable for analysis any time. Tap Analysis to reveal the progress, rank, radar and subject-wise charts.
About QTrack

QTrack is a simple study tracking platform designed to help users organise their work, monitor progress, and keep everything in one place. It supports all subjects and is made to work across devices so your data stays connected wherever you use it.

The goal of QTrack is to provide a clean and straightforward experience without unnecessary complexity.
